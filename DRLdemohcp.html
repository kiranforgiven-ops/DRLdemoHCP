The Focus list

Of this synthetic roster, which doctors rise to the top? Score each fictional HCP, check the estimate against brick-level sales, then rank and flag. This prototype contains 72 demo doctors; it does not reproduce the pitch deck’s 210-doctor example.

Step 1 · Score every doctor

Apply disclosed weights to synthetic category relevance, practice capacity, access, public signal and brand headroom.

Step 2 · Check against brick sales

Allocate each brick’s net addressable demand across its doctors; totals reconcile to the synthetic brick estimate.

Step 3 · Rank and flag

Compare relative priority and estimated potential separately. Every estimate includes a nominal interval and confidence flag.

Transparent allocation assumptions

Illustrative starting weights; sums are normalized within each brick.

Netted demand = brick category sales × addressable retail share. Doctor weights reflect category relevance, practice capacity, access and synthetic public-professional signals. Training audits adjust specialty multipliers, a separate calibration set adjusts interval width, and held-out records are excluded from both. This example does not model actual prescription behavior or infer referrals.

Brick totals reconcile

Every brick’s estimates sum to its net addressable total in each resample.

The 10th and 90th percentiles across 1,000 seeded perturbations form the displayed 80% interval. Wider intervals reflect greater input uncertainty. This is a prototype resampling procedure; calibration must be measured on a separate audit sample before operational use.

Deck arithmetic check

At the illustrative values above, ₹0.9 units × ₹38 contribution = ₹34.20 per redirected call. That is below a ₹420 cost per redirected call; simple per-call break-even would be 11.1 incremental units (₹420 ÷ ₹38), if those inputs are comparable.

The pitch’s larger effort figure (₹5.54 crore) and annual call volume need a defined time period, denominator and attribution model before they can be reconciled to this per-call example. Do not present either as validated ROI.
